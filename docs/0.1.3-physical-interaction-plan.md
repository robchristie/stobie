## Outcome

Close the physical-input qualification gap found in 0.1.2 without adding
application capability. A real pointer drag and mouse wheel must traverse the
platform, eframe and egui input path and produce the exact camera and virtual
gallery outcomes claimed by the vertical-slice report on native and WebGPU.
The reusable framework should own the drag and virtual-grid mechanics that made
the original application implementations easy to get wrong.

## Exploration question and exit condition

Question: what is the smallest framework-owned interaction model that preserves
the complete physical displacement, exact command history and a stable large
virtual scroll extent on both egui integrations?

Representative probes:

- a physical 90-by-50-point drag delivered as ten or more pointer movements;
- exact linked-camera equality after the drag and exact per-pane restoration
  after undo;
- physical wheel input over the 100,000-item thumbnail surface;
- retained scroll offset, advanced visible/materialised ranges and later
  demanded/resident keys;
- bounded presentation cache and bounded materialisation after scrolling;
- warmed idle and the existing release performance scenarios after the fixes.

Evidence owners are framework unit tests, Rust-owned snapshots captured around
real Playwright and xdotool input, structured scroll diagnostics, compositor
screenshots and the canonical native/browser smoke harnesses. Exploration exits
when both physical probes pass on both targets, the release observations are
requalified and `cargo xtask verify` succeeds.

## Selected boundaries

- `CameraDragSession` owns the exact starting camera set, total physical pointer
  displacement, linked preview and exact multi-pane completion command. The
  preview is always derived from the starting state, including movement before
  egui's drag-recognition threshold.
- `VirtualGridPresenter` in `polyorama-ui-egui` owns the exact scroll extent,
  retained offset, visible/materialised row geometry, positioned child UI,
  overscan and scroll diagnostics. The application pane owns thumbnail IDs,
  demands, cache lookup, painting and selection.
- Application diagnostics distinguish raw physical wheel events from frames in
  which the virtual-grid presenter consumed signed wheel movement.
- Semantic application controls remain for architectural assertions, but no
  physical-interaction acceptance claim may rely on them alone.

## Rejected paths

- Do not accept screenshot difference or undo-depth change as proof that the
  requested physical displacement occurred.
- Do not accept initial thumbnail residency as proof that a wheel changed the
  scroll state or demand range.
- Do not mutate the durable camera on every drag frame or accumulate floating-
  point camera transforms; derive one preview from the exact initial state.
- Do not allocate 100,000 widgets or introduce a second retained collection
  model to repair scrolling.
- Do not add widgets, analytical features, a render graph or a different GUI
  substrate during this pass.

## Delivery phases

1. Reproduce and map the two real-input failures.
2. Add framework-owned camera-drag and virtual-grid behaviour with focused
   tests.
3. Add honest scroll diagnostics and exact native/browser physical assertions.
4. Requalify screenshots, structured evidence, performance and the acceptance
   report through `cargo xtask verify`.
5. Independently review the exact committed head, repair blocking findings and
   land through one pull request.
